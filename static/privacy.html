Privacy Policy

Cloud Calculator (web and Android app) · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Cloud Calculator ("the app", "we") is a calculator made by Aarav and Rahil. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Google account details – when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture URL. We use these only to identify your account and show who is signed in.
  • Calculation history – expressions you calculate and their results, with timestamps, so your history can sync across your devices.
  • Usage counts – the number of calculations you have performed and your last sign-in time.

How we store it

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and in your device's browser storage (localStorage). The app is hosted on Google Cloud Run. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

How we use it

  • To provide and sync your calculation history.
  • To let the app administrator view aggregate usage statistics.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with third parties, other than the Google services used to run the app (Google Sign-In, Cloud Run, Firestore).

Deleting your data

You can delete individual history items or clear all history inside the app; this removes them from our servers. To delete your account record entirely, email rahilgupta017@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact
